# Contributing

Issues and pull requests are welcome. For geometry changes, include:

1. the design parameter being changed and the reason;
2. regenerated STEP/STL/geometry metadata;
3. `src/validate_outputs.py` results;
4. updated clearance/FOV evidence when the camera pose changes;
5. photographs or a clear statement that physical fit has not yet been tested.

Do not claim universal optimality from one manipulation task. Keep mechanical clearance, D405 working distance, optical reference, print tolerance, and cable sweep as separate acceptance criteria.
