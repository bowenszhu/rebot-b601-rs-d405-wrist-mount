A B601-RS-specific, open-hardware **30° downward eye-in-hand mount** for the [Seeed reBot Arm B601-RS](https://github.com/Seeed-Projects/reBot-DevArm) and [RealSense D405](https://www.realsenseai.com/products/stereo-depth-camera-d405/).

The design preserves Seeed's full D405 cradle and B601 wrist interface, then applies:

- **30° fixed downward pitch** — the largest integer pitch satisfying both tested clearance and working-distance boundaries at this fixed location;
- **9 mm lateral shift** — aligns the D405 left-imager optical reference, rather than the aluminium housing center, with the gripper centerline;
- **6 mm lift** — preserves conservative lower-front camera clearance as pitch increases;
- **solid lofted transition** — fills the side notch and creates a substantial load path;
- **unchanged handedness and USB side** — the cradle is not mirrored.

> “Best” means the best **validated general-purpose compromise under the stated B601-RS geometry and constraints**. It is not a universal optimum for every task or pose.

## D405 properties that drive the design

The RealSense D405 product page specifies an **ideal range of 7–50 cm**, **87° × 58° depth FOV**, global shutters, and RGB generated by the **left imager plus ISP**, without a separate RGB camera; see [“Small features, high impact → RGB without a dedicated RGB” and “Tech Specs → RGB → Technology”](https://www.realsenseai.com/products/stereo-depth-camera-d405/). The [D400 Calibration Tools User Guide, §2.1 “Calibration Parameters”](https://dev.realsenseai.com/docs/calibration-tools-user-guide-for-intel-realsense-d400-series/) states: “The left camera is the reference camera and is located at world origin.” These facts create three constraints:

1. see the gripper and near workspace without treating targets inside the 7 cm ideal near boundary as reliable depth;
2. align the left optical reference, which is not the center of the 42 mm housing;
3. prevent the lower-front camera corner from approaching the green gripper structure as pitch increases.

The official [D400 datasheet, Figure 10-13 on PDF page 146](https://realsenseai.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/Intel-RealSense-D400-Series-Datasheet-October-2025.pdf#page=146) supplies the 42 × 42 × 23 mm envelope, 20.00 mm rear M3 spacing, and 4.0 mm maximum insertion depth.

## Why 30°

A 20°–36° sweep at 1° increments used two task-independent boundaries:

- conservative D405-to-green-part clearance should remain approximately at or above the validated stock baseline (~9 mm in simulation, ~10 mm measured physically);
- optical reference to the TCP proxy should remain at least 70 mm, matching the D405's published ideal near-range boundary.

| Pitch | D405–green clearance | Optical reference–TCP proxy | Result |
| ---: | ---: | ---: | --- |
| 29° | 9.53 mm | 70.8 mm | feasible |
| **30°** | **9.12 mm** | **70.2 mm** | selected boundary solution |
| 31° | 8.71 mm | 69.6 mm | violates both conservative boundaries |

Interpolating ~30.3° would be false precision: print, seating, mesh, and calibration uncertainty are larger than 0.3°. Task-specific cube/tray pixel counts were examined but were **not** used as the general angle selector.

![MuJoCo angle and assembly comparison](docs/assets/simulation/wrist_mount_assembly_side_comparison.png)

## Why shift 9 mm

The [D405's 18 mm stereo baseline, shown on PDF page 146](https://realsenseai.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/Intel-RealSense-D400-Series-Datasheet-October-2025.pdf#page=146) places the left imager 9 mm from the stereo/housing midpoint. Keeping the Seeed handedness, the entire cradle moves **−9 mm in CAD X**:

```text
target left-imager X = 0
housing center X      = -9 mm
left-imager offset    = +9 mm
```

Seat, side/top wrap, bottom ledge, backplate, USB opening, and camera body move as one rigid system. Moving only the backplate would make the assembly inconsistent.

## Correcting the inherited 1 mm rear-hole offset

The pinned Seeed STEP has a 46 mm cradle centered at `X=0.00146 mm`, while the two rear holes are centered at `X=1.00146 mm`: a measured **+1.000 mm source offset**. [RealSense Figure 10-13, PDF page 146](https://realsenseai.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/Intel-RealSense-D400-Series-Datasheet-October-2025.pdf#page=146) specifies a centered 20.00 mm M3 pair on the 42 mm body.

The published design fills the inherited 3.2/5.6 mm passages, then cuts the corrected pair **−1.000 mm** at 3.6/6.0 mm print-clearance diameters. It retains the exact 20.000 mm spacing.

```bash
conda run -n rebot-d405-cad python src/inspect_source_hole_alignment.py
```

![D405 hole alignment correction](docs/assets/cad/d405-hole-alignment-correction.svg)

The 30° design was physically manufactured and installed; that installation exposed the source alignment issue. The published CAD includes the measured correction and passes single-solid, manifold-mesh, corrected-axis, passage, handedness, load-path, and D405-envelope checks.

![Physical D405 RGB and depth output, stock mount versus redesigned mount](docs/assets/camera/d405-physical-output-comparison.png)

The physical frames show the intended qualitative change: with the redesigned mount, the gripper occupies the image center and more of the near workspace, including the blue cube, enters the frame. They are **not a controlled quantitative experiment**: capture dates, illumination, and robot poses differed. The missing historical depth panel is deliberately left blank rather than reconstructed. ## Reproduce CAD and MuJoCo from the README

CAD uses its own Conda environment:

```bash
conda env create -f environment-cad.yml
conda activate rebot-d405-cad
python src/design_mount.py --angles 25 30 35
python src/validate_outputs.py build --angles 25 30 35
```

MuJoCo validation used [`ReBot_Arm_web_RS@d289fa7`](https://github.com/Yang-Ci/ReBot_Arm_web_RS/tree/d289fa7202ad7da9595a480b29397d70efacc4c1). Its robot assets are not vendored because that pinned commit exposes no repository-level license.

```bash
git clone https://github.com/Yang-Ci/ReBot_Arm_web_RS.git external/ReBot_Arm_web_RS
git -C external/ReBot_Arm_web_RS checkout d289fa7202ad7da9595a480b29397d70efacc4c1
python3 -m venv .venv-sim
.venv-sim/bin/pip install -r requirements-simulation.txt
export REBOT_MUJOCO_MODEL="$PWD/external/ReBot_Arm_web_RS/rebotarm_ros2/src/rebotarm_mujoco_rs/models/rs_grasp_scene.xml"
MUJOCO_GL=egl .venv-sim/bin/python simulation/simulate_fov.py
MUJOCO_GL=egl .venv-sim/bin/python simulation/sweep_angles.py
```

Detailed limitations are in [simulation.md](docs/simulation.md). MuJoCo is a screen, not physical collision certification or camera extrinsic calibration.

## Validation status

- 30° design physical manufacturing and installation: passed;
- corrected STEP: one valid solid;
- corrected STL: closed, connected, manifold;
- added adapter vs D405 envelope: `0.0000 mm³`;
- corrected source-hole fill volume: ~`212.1 mm³`;
- powered full-workspace collision sweep and long-duration thermal test: not claimed.

## Safety

- Disconnect robot motor power for first installation and manual clearance checks.
- D405 rear holes are `2× M3×0.5`, maximum insertion depth 4.0 mm. Check screw length against printed backplate thickness; never bottom a screw inside the camera.
- Seat the camera fully and tighten both screws gradually and alternately.
- Verify gripper opening, USB access, cable slack, and an unpowered wrist sweep before powered motion.
